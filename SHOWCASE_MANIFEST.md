## V0.4 public scope

V0.4 exposes, at a non-reconstructive level:

- the distinction between project authority, governance, capabilities, execution resources, derivatives and memory;
- the distinction between reusable core capabilities and governed project / method extensions;
- the model-agnostic execution strategy across frontier, local, deterministic and specialised resources;
- the separation between storage location, source authority and admitted reusable memory;
- the extended information → provenance → authority → derivation → action → trace chain;
- the comparative validation agenda and its current epistemic limits.

## Disclosure rule

A public showcase must demonstrate capability, architecture and evidence without exporting the private technical kit, partner deployment package, sensitive research-action material or reconstructive implementation contracts.

## Evidence rule

Observed usage, executable alpha components, specified architecture and research hypotheses are presented as different maturity classes.

V0.4 does not promote planned comparative experiments into completed proof.

## Repository contents

The public showcase contains no copied engine source, executable private runtime, partner traces or private corpora.
